# Plan verification, 2026-10-08

English version of [overeni-2026-10-08.md](overeni-2026-10-08.md), with a short update from the live tests on 2026-10-09 at the end.

**Short version.** Before the build we checked the plan's claims against sources. Each of six groups had a checker and a skeptic. Main corrections: a full run costs about 6 to 7 USD; city-level search exists already (Modash, Heepsy, hypebridge); checking ad labels exists already (Kolsquare, Modash); Meta's terms forbid automated collection even without login, so we do not claim compliance. The 2026-10-09 live tests then cut Meta Branded Content and showed that TikTok age and region are empty.

This is a check of the factual claims in the plan [brand-fit-cz.md](brand-fit-cz.md) (version 5, Czech; English version of v6: [plan.md](plan.md)) and the screens doc [kostra-aplikace-cz.md](kostra-aplikace-cz.md) (English: [screens.md](screens.md)) before the night build. Six groups. Each group had a first checker and an independent skeptic. Where the skeptic overruled the first conclusion, the skeptic's verdict stands. The fixes are in version 6 of both files. Actor details for the build (Czech): [apify-pro-krystofa.md](apify-pro-krystofa.md).

**How to read it.** Confirmed = the claim holds. Partly = it holds only in part, or misses an important detail. Refuted = it does not hold. Unverifiable = the source cannot be opened or does not exist. "Plan section N" and "plan difference N" point to [brand-fit-cz.md](brand-fit-cz.md). Tests T1 to T12 are defined in [apify-pro-krystofa.md](apify-pro-krystofa.md).

**What this report is based on.** On 2026-10-08 we ran no actor. Schemas, READMEs and price lists come from the public Apify API (`api.apify.com/v2/acts/...`), read on 2026-10-08. READMEs are undated; their examples are from 2024 to 2026. The live tests on 2026-10-09 are summarized in [Live test update](#live-test-update-2026-10-09). Where they differ from the tables, the live tests win.

## What it changes for the build

1. **Budget:** a full run costs about 6 to 7 USD; the Free credit is 5 USD. Starter or extra credits are a must, the cache is mandatory, and every run has a price cap.
2. **Round 2 without labels from the profile:** commercial saturation from captions (#reklama, #spoluprace, discount codes, @brands). A second post-scraper run in `detailedData` for 20 to 30 accounts only if T6 confirms `paidPartnership`. That run also gives `latestComments` and saves the comment-scraper.
3. **Location via `locationId`:** collect `locationId` values from hashtags, resolve them to coordinates, filter by Brno. `locationName` text is only a fallback. Filter places from search by coordinates.
4. **Minors:** no reliable age signal in public data. An explicit age under 18 in the bio = out at once, without storage. TikTok `isUnderAge18: true` = out. Otherwise age is a gap, and a finalist does not go into the report without the owner's check. `architecture.md` expects `is_under_18` from the provider; it will almost always be `None`.
5. **TikTok region:** do not use it until T10 shows it is filled and correct. `former_handles` in the model is, on IG, only the number of name changes.
6. **Missing data never eliminates:** empty `latestPosts` = fetch again. `PROFILE_EMPTY`, `AGE_RESTRICTED` and "not found in the Meta library" = a gap.
7. **Metrics:** only publicly visible likes and comments, hidden ones skipped, no views from the profile, pinned posts left out of activity.
8. **Data protection on load:** delete commenter identities from every actor (IG and TikTok, round 0 too). The Art. 10 filter also covers fines for administrative offences.
9. **Pitch and README:** do not claim "only we work at city level", "a new labeling check" or compliance with Meta's terms. Mention Modash Brno and hypebridge ourselves. Sentence for the jury: "We solve no CAPTCHA ourselves and write no scraper. We take public data through actors maintained by Apify, keep the minimum, and delete it after use."
10. **Validation (plan section 9):** use the public Modash Brno page and one pre-run hypebridge run as an outside comparison (read the Modash page by hand, do not scrape it).
11. **Features worth a few minutes of work:** an outreach draft with an explicit instruction to label the collaboration (barter too), and a step "check the published post". This is the business's defence under § 6b(3) of Act 40/1995 Coll. and § 21 of Act 250/2016 Coll. The chat's first message says it is an AI assistant (AI Act Art. 50).
12. **First hour:** tests T1 to T12 from [apify-pro-krystofa.md](apify-pro-krystofa.md) decide the cuts (Meta Branded Content, posts from places, TikTok, YouTube).
